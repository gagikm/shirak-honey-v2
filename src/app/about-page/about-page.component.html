Our Story

Four generations of beekeeping expertise, delivered straight from our hives to your table.

Beekeeping isn't just our business—it's our passion and heritage. For over four generations, our family has mastered the art of caring for bees and harvesting the purest honey nature has to offer.

Our journey began in the late 1800s in the historic region of Western Armenia, where our ancestors tended hives and perfected their beekeeping craft. Despite facing the hardships of the Armenian Genocide in 1915, the tradition survived and flourished in the Shirak province of Armenia, where our family continued to perfect their methods across generations.

In 2000, that rich heritage crossed the ocean to Sacramento, California. One year later, Shirak Honey was founded with a simple mission: to bring 100% raw, organic honey and authentic bee products to our community.

What makes our honey good?

  • Raw & Unfiltered: We never overheat or ultra-filter our honey, preserving all the natural enzymes, nutrients, and health benefits.
  • Locally Sourced: Our bees forage in Sacramento and surrounding areas including North Highlands, Lodi, and Rio Linda, creating truly local honey from local plants.
  • Family Tradition: Every jar represents generations of beekeeping knowledge and a deep respect for our bees.
  • Direct to You: From hive to jar to your table—no middlemen, no additives, just pure honey exactly as nature intended.

Since 2001, we've been proud to serve the Sacramento community with the finest raw honey and bee products. Whether you're drizzling it on your morning toast, sweetening your tea, or exploring its remarkable health benefits, you can trust that every drop of Shirak Honey carries the weight of four generations of expertise and care.
